About RentIt

Connecting people who need items with those who have them to rent

Our Story

RentIt was founded in 2023 with the vision of creating a sharing economy in Azerbaijan. We believe that instead of buying items you only need temporarily, you should be able to rent them from people in your community.

Our platform makes it easy to find what you need and rent it for the time you need it, saving you money and storage space.
